Counting College Staff web data (preview, built 2026-10-13)

Small aggregated CSVs read by the pages in substack/college-staffing/<tool>/.
Written by bloat/web_export/export_site_data.py in davidhummels/clauderesearch. Do not edit by hand; rerun the export.
Public data only: IPEDS (NCES), NSF HERD, EADA (U.S. Dept. of Education), USA Today NCAA finances, BLS LAUS, Census/SEER population.
Conventions: FY t staff = fall t-1 staff; S24 / S11 surviving matched sets; unweighted = mean of institution ratios (Delta's convention),
weighted = sum of staff / sum of FTE students. FY2024-25 IPEDS values are NCES provisional. Sector keys: pr public research, pm public
master's, pb public bachelor's, cc community colleges, xr private research, xm private master's, xb private bachelor's; sector groups:
rp regional publics (pm + pb), xmb private master's & bachelor's.
Staff measures (item 13): staffing results lead with faculty FTE and non-faculty FTE, FTE = full-time + part-time/3; faculty excludes
graduate assistants (GAs), which are a separate series. Pooled headcount (key total) is the v1/2014-brief measure, kept for the replication
and as a labeled comparison. PT faculty, GAs and the PT share are spliced at the FY2012|FY2013 HR redesign (drawn as separate segments;
changes start in FY2013); EAM/professional/nonprofessional are reclassified there. Community-college non-faculty FTE steps up about 1 log point (part-time staff) at FY2013,
so CC changes are measured from FY2013.

trends.csv: Staff per 1,000 fall FTE students by sector x FY x category (S24 surviving set); unw = mean of institution ratios, w = sum staff / sum FTE. v1 headcount keys: total, fte_staff, ftfac (FT faculty), ptfac (PT faculty incl. GAs), nonfac, eam, prof, nonprof, eamprof (= eam + prof). Item 13 FTE keys (FTE = FT + PT/3): facfte (faculty FTE excl. GAs), nonfacfte (non-faculty FTE), allftex (all FTE excl. GAs = facfte + nonfacfte), ptfacx (PT faculty excl. GAs, headcount), ga (graduate assistants, headcount), ptshare (PT share of faculty headcount excl. GAs, %; w = ratio of sums), facfte4 / facfte2 (faculty FTE at PT weight 1/4, 1/2). Not comparable across FY2012|FY2013: eam, prof, nonprof, eamprof (reclassified) and ptfacx, ga, ptshare, ptfac, total (contain PT faculty / GAs, spliced at the redesign); draw them as separate segments. CC non-faculty FTE has a step of about 1 log point (part-time staff) at FY2013. Source: bloat/substack/output/P1_per1000_annual_S24.csv; bloat/exhibits/output/A_split_per1000_annual_S24.csv. Years: FY1988-2002 even, FY2004-2012 even, FY2012-2025 annual (FY2024-25 NCES provisional).
replication.csv: Replication gate: staff per 1,000 FTE students from the DCP database (dcp), the clean IPEDS rebuild (clean) and the 2014 brief's Appendix Tables 1-3 (brief), DCP 26-year matched set, by sector x FY x measure. Source: bloat/staffing_panel/output/C_gate_appendix_tables_1_3.csv. Years: FY1990, 2000, 2010, 2012.
apptable1.csv: Appendix Table 1 extended: headcount and FTE staff per 1,000 FTE students (S24, mean of institution ratios) with the brief's values where they exist. Source: bloat/exhibits/output/AppTable1_Fig1_per1000_S24.csv. Years: FY1990, 2000, 2010, 2012, 2013, 2017, 2024, 2025.
finance_growth.csv: Real average annual % change (CAGR of the sector's mean real institution value, CPI-U FY) for six brief finance measures: DCP replication FY2002-10 with the brief's value, and the F13 extension FY2013-17, FY2017-24, FY2013-24. Source: bloat/exhibits/output/Fig6_Fig8_AppTable4_finance_growth.csv. Years: FY2002-2024 (FY2024 NCES provisional).
fte_index.csv: Total fall FTE students and index (FY1990 = 100) by sector and peer group, S24 surviving set. Source: bloat/substack/output/E1a_fte_index_annual_S24.csv; E5_peer_groups_annual_S24.csv. Years: FY1988-2025 (FY2024-25 NCES provisional).
fte_change.csv: FY2012->FY2024 fall FTE change by sector: share of institutions that shrank (S11), quantiles of institution log change, % change in sector total (S11: pct_change_total; S24: pct_s24_12_24). Source: bloat/substack/output/E4_growth_distribution_S11.csv; E1_enrollment_by_sector_S24.csv. Years: FY2012, FY2024 (NCES provisional).
decomp.csv: Staff-vs-students decomposition dlog(staff/FTE) = dlog staff - dlog FTE (log points x 100), S11, unweighted (u_, mean of institution log changes) and weighted (w_, group totals), by grouping; ver a = total FTE FY2012-24 (item 10), b = total FTE FY2013-24 (headline), c = on-campus FTE FY2013-24. ua/wa: per 1,000 at start; uab/wab at end. Measures: v1 headcount keys (total, fte_staff, ftfac, nonfac, eam, prof, nonprof, eamprof) and item 13 keys facfte, nonfacfte, allftex, ptfacx, ga, facfte4, facfte2 (item 13 rows only where the window is within one HR era or the measure contains no PT faculty or GAs: ver a only for nonfacfte; GA rows are research-sector only, small n). The v1 ver a rows for total, nonfac and fte_staff are kept unchanged but are cross-era (they contain PT faculty / GAs spliced at FY2012|13); the site shows ver a only for nonfacfte and ftfac. Source: bloat/online/output/D1_decomposition_oncampus_S11.csv; D1_split_decomposition_S11.csv. Years: FY2012/FY2013 -> FY2024 (NCES provisional).
online_share.csv: Share of fall headcount enrolled exclusively online (exc_share_hc) and online share of FTE, by sector x FY, S24; unw = mean of institution shares, w = ratio of sums. Source: bloat/online/output/T1_online_share_by_sector_fy.csv. Years: FY2013-2025 (falls 2012-2024; falls 2020-21 COVID; FY2025 NCES provisional).
dual_sector.csv: Dual-enrollment proxy (under-18 undergraduates converted to FTE) as a share of fall FTE, by sector group: mean of institution shares and ratio of sums. Source: bloat/causal/output/e_dual_by_sector.csv. Years: falls 2005, 2013, 2023.
dual_decline.csv: FY2014->FY2024 log change (x100) in fall FTE including and excluding dual-enrollment FTE, staff, and staff per FTE, by sector group (S11) and staff measure (measure: total = v1 headcount; facfte, nonfacfte, allftex, ptfacx, ga, facfte4, facfte2 = item 13; filter on measure). The dual-enrollment gap is a denominator effect, the same for every measure. Source: bloat/causal/output/e_decline_net_of_dual.csv; e_decline_net_of_dual_split.csv. Years: FY2014, FY2024 (NCES provisional).
inst_index.csv: One row per institution (extension group; DCP-era systems kept as one unit): name (the group's own campus), number of IPEDS campuses (unitids) mapped to the group, state, sector key, peer group (flag/reg/small), academic medical center class (hosp = owns a hospital, med = medical school without hospital), set membership (s24, s11), fall FTE FY2013 and FY2024, FY2013->FY2024 log change x100 in total headcount staff (d_staff, v1), FTE and headcount staff per FTE (d_ratio, v1), on-campus FTE (S11), and flags for finance/athletics data; item 13: FY2013->FY2024 log change x100 in faculty FTE (d_facfte) and non-faculty FTE (d_nonfacfte) and in each per FTE student (d_facfte_r, d_nonfacfte_r). Source: bloat/staffing_panel/output/staff_ext_groups_fy1988_2025.csv.gz; staff_split_ext_groups_fy1988_2025.csv.gz; bloat/substack/output/group_attributes.csv; bloat/amc/output/amc_classification.csv; bloat/online/output/D0b_institution_frame_S11.csv. Years: FY2013, FY2024 (NCES provisional).
inst/<ST>.csv: one row per institution x FY for institutions in that state: fall FTE students; staff per 1,000 FTE students (v1 headcounts: tot total headcount, ftf FT faculty, ptf PT faculty incl. GAs, eam, pro professional, npr nonprofessional, nfc non-faculty, ep EAM + professional; item 13: ffte faculty FTE excl. GAs (FT + PT/3), nfte non-faculty FTE, ptx PT faculty excl. GAs, ga graduate assistants, afx all FTE excl. GAs; pts PT share of faculty headcount excl. GAs, %; not comparable across FY2012|FY2013: eam/pro/npr/ep (reclassified) and ptx/ga/pts/ptf/tot (spliced PT faculty and GAs)); spending per FTE student in real FY2024 $ by function, hospitals excluded (ins instruction, acs academic support, sts student services, iss institutional support, aux auxiliary enterprises; FY2004+); ath EADA athletic expense per FTE student (real FY2024 $, FY2007+). Source: bloat/staffing_panel/output/staff_ext_groups_fy1988_2025.csv.gz; staff_split_ext_groups_fy1988_2025.csv.gz; bloat/causal/output/f_panel.csv.gz; data/external/athletics/eada_institution_fy2007_2024.csv.gz. Years: FY1988-2025 (staff), FY2004-2024 (finance), FY2007-2024 (athletics); FY2024-25 NCES provisional.
bands.csv: Sector peer bands: 25th, 50th and 75th percentile across all institutions in the sector reporting that FY, for every institution-file measure (same codes as inst/<ST>.csv). Source: same as inst/<ST>.csv. Years: FY1988-2025.
states.csv: By state x control/level (pub4, pub2, priv4, all), S24 surviving set: number of institutions, fall FTE FY1990/2000/2012/2024 and % change FY2012-24; staff (total headcount) per 1,000 FTE students FY2012 and FY2024, unweighted (pk) and weighted (pkw), computed here from the item-3 panel; state dual-enrollment intensity (public under-18 undergraduates per 100 residents aged 15-17, falls 2013 and 2023; same value on every row of a state). Item 13: faculty FTE (fac_), non-faculty FTE (nonfac_) and all FTE excl. GAs (allx_) per 1,000 FTE students, FY2013 and FY2024, unweighted (_pk_) and weighted (_pkw_); FY2013 base because PT faculty is spliced at FY2012|13. Source: bloat/substack/output/E2_enrollment_by_state_S24.csv; bloat/staffing_panel/output/staff_ext_groups_fy1988_2025.csv.gz; staff_split_ext_groups_fy1988_2025.csv.gz; bloat/causal/output/e_dual_state_intensity.csv. Years: FY1990-2024 (FY2024 NCES provisional).
decline_decomp.csv: Gravity-instrument decomposition of the FY2013->FY2024 change in log fall FTE (x100) into population (demographic), national cycle (upper bound), local cycle and residual; online_offset = dlog total FTE - dlog on-campus FTE; dlog_staff = total headcount. Sample: 1,903 S11 groups with the instrument (sector x FY effects), unweighted and FY2013-FTE weighted. Source: bloat/causal/output/c_decomposition.csv. Years: FY2013-FY2024 (NCES provisional).
staff_split.csv: Rise in staff per student FY2013->FY2024 (x100 log points) on the 1,903-group gravity sample, split into the part due to an elasticity below 1 meeting the enrollment change [(b_demog-1) x population part + (b_other-1) x other change] and staff growth beyond enrollment (the remainder). measure: total (v1 headcount), nonfac (v1 non-faculty headcount), facfte, facfte4, facfte2, ftfac, ptfacx, nonfacfte, allftex, ga. method: cf = pooled control-function elasticities (v1 method; headline), fe / iv = pooled within-institution OLS / 2SLS (b2002) elasticity, fe_sec / iv_sec = sector-specific elasticities (b_source 'pooled (sector F < 10)' = fell back to pooled). gfe: ext_group (main) or group_era (institution x HR-era effects). level: all, sector (7 sectors), sgroup (rp, cc, xmb sector groups). CF rows apply pooled elasticities to sector means. weights: unweighted (all measures); FY2013 FTE (v1 headcount CF only). Headline: faculty FTE 11.0 = 7.1 + 3.9; non-faculty FTE 17.3 = 9.6 + 7.8; headcount 12.8 = 7.7 + 5.1. Source: bloat/causal/output/m_split_staff_split.csv; c_decomposition.csv; c_staff_response.csv. Years: FY2013-FY2024 (NCES provisional).
elasticities.csv: 2SLS elasticity of staff to fall FTE, gravity population instrument (county base b1987 main, b2002 robustness), by sector group and staff category, with first-stage F and the within-institution FE (OLS) coefficient; 'All' = pooled (sector x FY effects). v1 headcount outcomes: total, ftfac (FT faculty; identical on FTE), nonfac, nonprof, eamprof. Item 13 outcomes: facfte, nonfacfte, allftex, ptfacx, ga, ptshare (PT share of faculty, level not log), facfte4, facfte2, eamproffte, eamfte, proffte, nonproffte. gfe = ext_group (institution effects) or group_era (institution x HR-era effects); main = 1 marks the row the site shows (group_era for spliced or reclassified measures). GA rows are noisy and sign-unstable. Rows with fs_F < 20 (the private sectors, F about 11-14) have weak first stages: the site does not show them and they should not be quoted. Source: bloat/causal/output/d_staff_by_sector.csv; c_panel_iv.csv; m_split_sector_elasticities.csv; m_split_elasticities.csv. Years: FY1990-2024 panel (b1987), FY2006-2024 (b2002).
gravity_paths.csv: Cumulative change in log fall FTE since FY2013 (x100), actual vs the part predicted by the gravity population instrument (population = pi_Z,sector x change in Z) and the national cycle (b_u,sector x change in the national unemployment rate), by sector, year by year; FY2024 values equal decline_decomp.csv. Same 1,903-group sample; groups present in each year (computed here). Source: bloat/causal/output/c_enrollment_eq.csv, c_cycle_timeseries.csv, c_gravity_ext_group_fy.csv.gz; bloat/models/output/analysis_panel_fy1988_2025.csv.gz; data/external/bls_laus. Years: FY2013-FY2024 (NCES provisional).
online_providers.csv: Test of the national-online-provider hypothesis: log fall FTE on state exposure to the 51 national online providers (X) and its interactions with a non-traditional campus profile (X_NT) and local draw (X_LOC), by sector group. The hypothesis predicts negative X_NT and X_LOC; it is not supported. Source: bloat/causal/output/d_online_models.csv. Years: FY2013-2024.
occupations.csv: Non-medical FTE staff (FT + PT/3) per 1,000 FTE students by IPEDS occupation group, FY2015 and FY2024 (S11, mean of institution ratios), log change x100 in staff, FY2015 share and contribution to the log change in all staff. enrfacing = studaff + commsport + sales. Item 13 rows: noninstr (all non-instructional FTE = allstaff - instr); instr_ft, instr_pt, noninstr_ft, noninstr_pt, allstaff_ft, allstaff_pt are headcounts (FT or PT) per 1,000. Note: instructional (EAP 210) is not the same as faculty, and non-instructional includes research/public-service (220/230) staff. Source: bloat/causal/output/f_composition.csv; f_split_composition.csv. Years: FY2015-FY2024 (NCES provisional).
coaches.csv: Coaches (EADA coach FTE) as a share of the FY2015->FY2024 growth in enrollment-facing staff (total FTE and per 1,000 students), by sector group; a lower bound on athletics staffing. Source: bloat/causal/output/g_coaches_share.csv. Years: FY2015-FY2024.
occ_elasticity.csv: Elasticity of staff in each occupation group to fall FTE: 2SLS with the gravity instrument (b2002) and within-institution FE, by sector group. Item 13 rows: noninstr (non-instructional FTE), instr_ft (FT instructional headcount). Source: bloat/causal/output/f_occ_elasticity.csv; f_split_occ_elasticity.csv. Years: FY2015-2024.
enrfacing_share.csv: Change FY2015->FY2024 in FTE staff per 1,000 students (S11, mean of institution ratios): enrollment-facing, all non-instructional, instructional, all staff, FT and PT instructional (headcount); enrollment-facing share of the rise in non-instructional staff (headline, 61% for all) and of all staff (v1 measure, 45%). Private research share is not meaningful (non-instructional rose only 0.5 per 1,000). Source: bloat/causal/output/f_split_enrfacing_share.csv. Years: FY2015-FY2024 (NCES provisional).
amenity_growth.csv: Median log change x100 in real spending per FTE student: amenity (student services + auxiliaries), academic (instruction + academic support), student services, instruction; hospitals excluded; by sector group. Source: bloat/causal/output/f_spending_growth.csv. Years: FY2004-FY2024 and FY2013-FY2024 (NCES provisional).
athletics_growth.csv: Median log change x100 in real EADA athletic expense per FTE student vs academic spending per FTE, median athletic $ per FTE at start and end (real FY2024 $), and athletics as a share of amenity spending, by sector group. Source: bloat/causal/output/g_athletics_growth.csv. Years: FY2008-FY2024 and FY2013-FY2024.
athletics_share.csv: Athletics' share of the growth in amenity and non-instructional spending (real $, dollar-weighted): athletics opex excludes athletic aid, total includes it; $bn changes; median school-level share. Source: bloat/causal/output/g_athletics_share_of_spending.csv. Years: FY2008-FY2024 and FY2013-FY2024.
d1_subsidy.csv: Division I public universities (USA Today): student fees + institutional support to athletics ('subsidy') per FTE student, real $, and as a share of athletic revenue; log growth x100 vs instruction per FTE. Source: bloat/causal/output/g_d1_subsidy.csv. Years: FY2006-FY2022 and FY2013-FY2022.
payoff.csv: Does growth in spending (or staff) over FY2015->19 predict growth in first-time students over FY2019->24? Elasticities (SE) by spec: A academic + amenity; B academic + non-athletic amenity + athletics; A_athsample A on B's sample; C academic + non-instructional; D academic + institutional support; E enrollment-facing staff (11b-j). Controls: gravity instrument change in both windows, prior own trend; sector FE pooled; SEs clustered by state. Associations only. Source: bloat/causal/output/g_spending_payoff.csv; f_payoff.csv. Years: FY2015-FY2024.
kpis.csv: Every headline number shown on the overview and KPI cards, with the repo source file and column it comes from. Source: see source and column fields. Years: as labelled.
